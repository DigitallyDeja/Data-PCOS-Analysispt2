# Data-PCOS-Analysispt2
This is a follow up to the Data-PCOS-Analysis part one project. This project is utilizing the previous Excel data and it is extracted through Tableau. Also, the KPIs differ from the Excel PCOS interactive dashboard as it shows a unique angle into the data's findings.

## Questions KPIs
### Summary KPIs
- What is the total of participants?
    
### Core KPIs
- What is the PCOS Status among participants (Yes, No, Not diagnosed)?
- Does exercising (Exerciser, Non-exerciser) have a contribution to heart disease for PCOS participants?
- Is there a correlation with the participants' mental health and hormonal imbalances?
- How does taking PCOS medication affect conception amongst different age groups?

## Project Insights 
- There are 173 participants in total across all age groups.
- 21.97% of participants have PCOS in the dataset, while 77.46% do not.
- According to the data ~21% of PCOS participants that do not exercise are diagnosed with heart disease, and less than 3% who exercise is diagnosed. Whereas ~95% of PCOS participants exercise, the data shows that exercise drastically decreases the likelihood of heart disease.
- 92% of PCOS particpants that have hormonal imbalances struggle with mental health, this is a strong indicator that PCOS affects mental health
